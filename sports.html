PhilSA Sports

PhilSA competes in a multitude of sports semester round! These sports consist of basketball, volleyball, soccer, flag football, ultimate frisbee, and a mystery sport for each year's Goodphil! As for competition, teams compete in the university's intramural (IM) games and Goodphil games. To spectate any PhiLSA intramural games, view the IM schedule here (link TBD).

Get Involved

Want to join Sports?

PhilSA builds a community around activies such as sports. Playing a sport with PhilSA doesn't require anything! All you have to do is join the specific sport's GroupMe and be caught up with practice announcements.

To compete in one of these sports for IM games, sign up through Texas A&M's IM website. In order to play during Goodphil, you must be a member and meet your point requirements. Teams may also have tryouts to be part of the team.
