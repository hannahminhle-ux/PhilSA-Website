How points work

Point Requirements

Points are earned by attending and participating in PhilSA events throughout the semester. To participate in Isang Mahal and/or Goodphil, the points requirement must be met:

POINT MAXES: Modern: 40 points, Sports: 40 points

ISANG MAHAL

  • 6 Cultural Points
  • 3 Philanthropy Points
  • 3 Fundraising Points
  • 24 Total Points

GOODPHIL

Full Year Members

  • 20 Cultural Points
  • 10 Philanthropy Points
  • 10 Fundraising Points
  • 80 Total Points

Half Year Members

  • 10 Cultural Points
  • 5 Philanthropy Points
  • 5 Fundraising Points
  • 60 Total Points
