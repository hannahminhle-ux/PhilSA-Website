About the team

What is Cultural?

Cultural Events

Interested in learning more about the Philippines? Cultural events are hosted to teach everything about Philippine and Filipino-American culture. From the history, to the food, to the music, you're bound to learn more about the "Pearl of the Orient!"

Cultural Dance

PhilSA Cultural is a three-peat winner of the Goodphil cultural competition since 2024. The team celebrates the beauties of many cultural dances spanning from every corner of the Philippines. Every year, the team performs at Isang Mahal in the fall and Goodphil in the spring. Anyone is welcome to join the team regardless of dance experience or ethnic background!

Get Involved

Want to join the Cultural team?

Keep a look out on Instagram @philsacultural for updates on when workshops are. Two open workshops are held at the beginning of each semester, so make sure to attend those before they close. For any questions/inquiries, DM @philsacultural on Instagram.
