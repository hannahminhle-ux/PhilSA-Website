PhilSA Philippine Student Association

Texas A&M PhilSA is the first Filipino Student Association out of the thirteen FSA universities across Texas and Oklahoma.
Being one of the largest Asian organizations at A&M, PhilSA encourages those from all backgrounds to join and take part in learning about Filipino culture!
Here, we emphasize community, inclusivity, diversity, and love through sports, culture, dance, competition, and "phamily"!

How to Join PhilSA

Philsa does not have an application form! All you have to do to become a member and obtain membership privileges is pay dues! Dues are listed below with their respective due dates. Past the due date, you will be unable to receive membership benefits including the ones listed below!

BENEFITS

  1. Participate in Goodphil and Isang Mahal performances
  2. Participate in Goodphil sports
  3. Participate in phams

DUES

  • Full year: $65
  • Fall semester (half year): $45

ALL DUES MUST BE PAID BY OCTOBER 9TH!

Venmo @bankofvansh or pay through Flywire & provide your full name and t-shirt size!
