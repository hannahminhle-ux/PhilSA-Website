Goodphil

A four-day competition bringing together Filipino Student Associations from across Texas and Oklahoma to go head-to-head.

About the competition

One weekend, four days, one champion

The Goodphil Games is a four day conference and competition that unites all thirteen of the Filipino Student Associations. Here, we go head-to-head in three different categories of dance and individual sports.

It is a huge annual event held in the spring semester that rotates between one of five designated host colleges every year!
